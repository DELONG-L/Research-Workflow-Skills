# Cover prompts

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Prompts 1–3 establish the composition, scale, and Chinese localization. Prompts 4–5 apply the final book-serif typography to the English and Chinese editions. Both covers are conceptual illustrations, not screenshots or research results.

## 1. English composition

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an editorial GitHub README cover for the open-source project Research Workflow Skills. Create one wide landscape banner, approximately 2400 x 1000 pixels, aspect ratio 2.4:1. Subject: two independently usable research skills: experiment design and management, and research workspace governance. The visual metaphor is an orderly research desk drawn as a precise, airy flat editorial illustration, NOT a product screenshot. Color direction: cool off-white #F3F6F8 background, slate-blue #355469 for titles and principal lines, deep blue-gray #243746, pale steel-blue #DFE7ED, restrained copper #AA7550 for small marks only. This should feel like a thoughtful research field guide: calm, exact, spacious, quietly sophisticated. Left 40 percent: exact project title on TWO lines, 'Research' then 'Workflow Skills', in NORMAL, unmodified, regular-weight humanist sans-serif typography comparable to Avenir Next Regular. Moderate size, about 92px at a 2400px canvas width, balanced line spacing about 1.25, natural kerning. Below with a generous gap, two small subtitle lines exactly: 'Reproducible experiments.' and 'Clear workspace boundaries.' All text must be perfectly straight, horizontal, crisp, correctly spelled and undistorted. Never stretch, compress, skew, warp or curve letters. No condensed font, heavy bold, calligraphy, extreme stroke contrast, or oversized title. Outer margins at least 120px. Right 54 percent: TWO DISTINCT HORIZONTAL VISUAL ROWS, with generous vertical separation and NO connecting arrow between rows. Upper row has the exact normal sans-serif label 'Experiments' and a minimal sequence of three thin upright research note sheets, linked by a fine line: one shows a simple plan outline, one shows sparse aligned log rows, one shows source-linked notes. No numerical charts, performance results or invented research. Lower row has the exact normal sans-serif label 'Workspaces' and an orderly folder/map illustration: a clearly bounded local folder with a simple directory branch linking separate small document/code folders. The two rows are complementary and independently meaningful, not sequential steps or a forced hierarchy. All small text-like details are generic straight line rules; no tiny fake words, file paths, authors, logos or platform brands. Use subtle paper edges, minimal depth, precise line work and large amounts of untouched space; no thick cards or app UI. Keep the composition balanced and comfortable at GitHub README width. Only the specified title, subtitles and two row labels are readable text. No decorative grids across the background, no gradients, no 3D lettering, no botanical art, no watermark.
```
